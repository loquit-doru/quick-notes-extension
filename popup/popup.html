Privacy & Trust

  • Notes storage: Your notes are stored locally in your browser.
  • Note content: Quick Notes does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your note content.
  • Page context: If enabled, Quick Notes may save the current page title and URL with your note. This stays local.
  • Usage insights: Optional local-only funnel counters (install/first open/first note/reminder/upgrade/restore) stored on this device.
  • Cloud sync: No cloud sync.
  • Payments: If you upgrade to Pro, payment providers may process payment-related information. Quick Notes does not store card details.

Store minimal funnel events on this device only (no note text, no network telemetry).

Permissions

  • storage — save notes and settings on this device
  • activeTab — optionally attach the current page after you open the extension
  • alarms — schedule reminders you set
  • notifications — reminder alerts and optional update notices
