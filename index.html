Quick Notes

Built for people who work in browser tabs all day: capture ideas quickly, keep notes local, and upgrade to Pro only if you outgrow free limits.

Add to Chrome Add to Edge

Why Quick Notes

  • Capture notes without switching to a full notes app
  • No account required — start immediately
  • Notes stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB)
  • Optional page URL and title saved with each note
  • Core flow stays free; Pro is for heavier daily use

Free

  • 7-day full access trial on install
  • Then up to 10 notes, any length
  • Export JSON, Markdown, and plain text
  • Reminders and themes

Pro (one-time purchase)

  • Unlimited notes
  • Search, folders, and PIN lock
  • Longer trash retention and on-device auto-backup

Pricing shown in the extension upgrade screen at purchase time.

Privacy

  • Notes stay locally in your browser — not on our servers
  • We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share note content
  • We do not track browsing history
  • We do not use analytics
  • Optional page context is saved locally with your note only when you enable it
  • Pro payments may use ExtensionPay/Stripe and license restore APIs — we do not store card details
