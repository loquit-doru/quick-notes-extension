Quick Notes - Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 16, 2026

TL;DR: Your notes stay on your device. We do not receive your note text unless you use optional payment/license checks (email or transaction hash only).

Data Storage

  • Notes, folders, and trash: IndexedDB (local)
  • Settings, reminders, trial, PIN: chrome.storage.local
  • No cloud sync by default

Permissions

PermissionPurpose
storageLocal settings and reminder data
activeTab / tabsOptional page context (URL & title) on new notes
alarmsSchedule note reminders
notificationsShow reminder alerts

Third-Party Services

  • ExtensionPay — Card payments (Stripe). We do not store card details.
  • License API — quick-notes-pro.apiworkersdev.workers.dev — Crypto verify / license restore only (email and/or tx hash; not note contents).
  • Base Network — Public transaction verification for crypto payments.
